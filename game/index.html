HOW TO PLAY

Goal. You are Amber (attack). Carry the charge to Site A — Foundry — or Site B — Yard, hold E to plant, then defend it for 40 seconds. Or eliminate all five Cobalt defenders. Cobalt wins a round by stopping the plant until the clock dies, defusing a planted charge, or wiping Amber.

Money. Kills $300, planting $300, round win $3,250, loss $1,400. Press B in the buy phase for rifles, SMGs, a sniper, a shotgun, armor and smoke.

Gunplay. First shots are laser-true; spraying blooms the crosshair and the recoil climbs. Stop, crouch, tap. Headshots deal double. Armor halves damage. Moving fast widens your spread — Shift-walk to stay quiet and accurate.

Bots. Your four Amber teammates push sites with you; Cobalt holds angles, rotates on gunfire and rushes a planted charge to defuse. Smoke (G) blocks their sight.
